# Command Center App — overview

Internal tooling project — not a customer-facing business; no social content applicable.

The 3D city dashboard at localhost:8777: neon island-block city rendered in Three.js, driven entirely by real vault data. Agents appear as named characters with light beacons; buildings are projects; population = real leads + content + council output. Tabs: Overview, Advertising & Content, Money & Leads, Subagents, Activity. The Approve/Hold queue writes real approval stamps into vault files, and hiring approvals create real Claude Code subagent files.

Source: scratchpad/command-center/ (server.py, city.js, app.js, index.html, style.css).
