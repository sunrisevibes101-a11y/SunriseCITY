# Website Building Service — overview

Real chosen business (selected via full council deliberation on 2026-07-26 over AI phone-answering and AI video ads). Agents find small businesses with no/poor websites, send automated outreach with an auto-quote, build the site once confirmed, and a human (owner + main agent) verifies before delivery and payment.

**Status: pre-launch.** Blocking items before any outreach can start (from the founding council session):
1. Dedicated sending domain, warmed, with CAN-SPAM compliance (unsubscribe, physical address, opt-out) -- OWNER action, not something agents can do autonomously (domain registration = a purchase).
2. Real payment flow (Stripe or equivalent) -- OWNER action (account creation).
3. Lead list (50-100 targets), quote logic, build/deploy pipeline -- AGENT action, buildable now.

Success metric: NOT a single conversion percentage (a flawed >5-8% cold-outreach target was explicitly overruled during founding deliberation). Track open rate, reply rate, and quote rate as separate funnel stages instead.
