# Which business to start first: phone-answering vs. video ads vs. website-building

## Question

Comparing three candidate first businesses for an AI-agent workforce: (1) AI phone-answering service for small businesses, (2) AI-generated video ads via Higgsfield, (3) website-building service for businesses with no/poor websites.

## Chairman's recommendation

**Build the website-building business first** — capability already proven, lowest technical barrier, and a competent autonomous agent workforce (lead-find → quote → build → close) is a real differentiator even in a saturated market.

**Overruled:** the strategist's >5-8% quote-to-paid conversion target — that's a warm/inbound benchmark; real cold-outreach funnels net well under 1% of total contacts to paid. Replaced with tracking open/reply/quote rates separately rather than one blended conversion number.

**Kept:** websites as the wedge; deprioritizing video ads for now (Higgsfield requires an owner-maintained paid account, breaking the "pure agent workforce" thesis, and risks disintermediation).

**Top risk carried forward (not resolved, must be solved week one):** CAN-SPAM compliance and sending-domain deliverability are unverified and unbuilt. A burned domain or compliance violation could stall the whole pilot before it produces one data point.

**Next actions:**
1. Owner: register/warm a dedicated sending domain, confirm CAN-SPAM requirements (unsubscribe, physical address, opt-out) — this week.
2. Agents: build lead list (50-100 targets), quote logic, build/deploy pipeline in parallel.
3. Owner: set up a real payment flow (Stripe or equivalent) for closing deals.
4. Agents: run the first 50-100 cold-lead sprint, tracking funnel stages separately.
5. Owner + council: review at 2 weeks; decide whether to scale, or cheaply test the Higgsfield disintermediation question before fully shelving video ads.

**Would change this recommendation:** if deliverability/compliance setup takes materially longer/costs more than expected — that alone could justify starting with phone-answering instead, since its cost structure is better understood upfront.

## Real market data (researcher)

- **Phone answering**: crowded — 9-10 established competitors (Smith.ai, Rosie, Ruby, Goodcall, My AI Front Desk), $24.95-$292.50/mo range. Real Twilio integration needed, raw usage $0.08-0.15/min.
- **Video ads (Higgsfield)**: needs paid Creator/Studio tier for commercial rights. Comparable agency pricing is $1000-2500/mo, so $1000/mo target is in-range. No named direct competitor doing this exact resale model (could be gap or could be no margin).
- **Websites**: severely saturated — $46.7B US market, 203,000+ firms. Durable/Wix/10Web compete directly, some free/near-free. Lowest technical barrier since site-building capability already proven.

## Critic's sharpest catch

The strategist's ">5-8% quote-to-paid on first 50-100 cold leads" target was flagged as unrealistic — that's an inbound/warm-lead conversion benchmark being misapplied to cold outreach, where realistic funnels net well under 1% of total contacts to paid. This was overruled by the chairman rather than used as a go/no-go gate.
