How A Project Goes

Quote to done, in four honest steps

No sales pressure, no vague allowances buried in the fine print. Here's exactly what to expect when you work with us.

  1. 01

    Free Consultation

    We walk the space with you, talk through your goals and budget, and answer questions. No pressure, no obligation to move forward.

  2. 02

    Written Proposal

    You get a clear scope of work and pricing in writing before anything is signed — what's included, what it costs, and when it happens.

  3. 03

    Scheduled Build

    Once you approve the plan, we schedule the crew and keep you updated at each stage — materials, timeline, and any changes along the way.

  4. 04

    Final Walkthrough

    We walk the finished project with you, handle the punch list, and don't call the job done until you're satisfied with the result.
