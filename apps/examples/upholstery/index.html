What We Do

Four ways we bring furniture back to life

Furniture Reupholstery

Sofas, armchairs, dining chairs, and headboards get fresh fabric and new padding where it's needed, with frames checked and tightened along the way.

Custom Cushions & Pillows

Bench seats, window seats, and outdoor furniture fitted with cushions cut to your exact dimensions, in fabric and fill weight you choose.

Antique & Heirloom Restoration

Older pieces get careful attention — original details preserved where possible, structural repairs handled before a single stitch of new fabric goes on.

Fabric Selection & Sourcing

In-studio swatch consultations to help you pick a fabric that fits the piece, your space, and how the furniture actually gets used day to day.

What to Expect

A straightforward process, start to finish

  1. 1

    Free Estimate

    Send photos or set up an in-studio visit. We'll look at the piece and give you an honest estimate before any work begins.

  2. 2

    Fabric Consultation

    Browse swatches in the studio and talk through weight, pattern, and durability for how the piece will actually be used.

  3. 3

    Teardown & Frame Check

    We carefully strip the old fabric and padding and check the frame, springs, and joints, flagging anything that needs repair first.

  4. 4

    Hand Reupholstery

    New padding and fabric are fitted, cut, and finished by hand — the same care whether it's a footstool or a full sofa.

  5. 5

    Final Walkthrough

    We go over the finished piece together before it leaves the studio, so you know exactly what was done.
